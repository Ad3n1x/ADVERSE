ADVERSE by AD3N1X

Evidence-led public-source investigative research

ADVERSE is an independent research workspace created by AD3N1X. It helps researchers organise investigations, discover public-source material, preserve source provenance, and connect entities, events, documents and relationships into a structured evidence trail.

Research and evidence tools

  • Search configured public-source providers and review source results.
  • Organise investigations, evidence records, entities and relationships.
  • Review source provenance and distinguish publication, capture and retrieval dates.
  • Explore relationship graphs and historical archive records.
  • Compare evidence and organise candidate claims for human review.
  • Use AI-assisted evidence review as a provisional aid, not a truth detector.
  • Export investigation material in supported formats.

Evidence limitations

ADVERSE does not guarantee exhaustive web coverage or determine truth automatically. Search coverage depends on configured providers. A reachable URL or graph connection is not proof of truth or a relationship. Researchers should verify conclusions against original sources.
