ADVERSE by AD3N1X

Frequently asked questions

ADVERSE is an independent evidence-led public-source investigative research workspace created by Adeniran Victor Adebowale, who publishes software as AD3N1X.

What is ADVERSE?

ADVERSE helps researchers organise investigations, discover public-source material through configured providers, preserve source provenance and connect entities, events, documents and relationships into a structured evidence trail.

Does ADVERSE search the whole internet?

No. Search coverage is bounded by the configured providers, provider access, rate limits and availability. It is not a universal web crawler and does not guarantee exhaustive results.

How is source provenance handled?

Users can retain source URLs, excerpts and provenance context while keeping publication, capture and retrieval dates distinct.

Do relationship graphs prove connections?

No. Graphs help researchers navigate recorded entities and relationships. Every meaningful conclusion should be checked against its underlying evidence.

Does AI review establish truth?

No. AI-assisted reviews and claim triage are provisional aids. They do not replace source verification or human judgement.

What are the responsible-use boundaries?

ADVERSE is intended for lawful public-source research and bounded monitoring of assets that the user owns or is explicitly authorised to monitor. It is not a general-purpose private-network scanner.

Are investigations public?

No. Investigation records and research tools belong to the authenticated workspace. The public homepage and this FAQ are the public product references.
