ADVERSE by AD3N1X

What is ADVERSE by AD3N1X?

ADVERSE by AD3N1X is an evidence-led public-source investigative research workspace. It helps researchers organise investigations, discover public-source material, preserve source provenance and connect entities, events, documents and relationships in a structured evidence trail.

What does ADVERSE do?

ADVERSE brings public-source discovery and evidence organisation into one authenticated research workspace. Users can save source records and excerpts, retain original URLs, distinguish publication, capture and retrieval dates, map recorded entities and relationships, review historical archive material, organise candidate claims and export investigation records.

How does ADVERSE use AI?

Optional AI-assisted evidence review can help organise and examine stored evidence. Its output is provisional and requires human verification against original sources. ADVERSE is not an automatic truth detector, and its AI review does not independently browse or verify every original URL.

Does ADVERSE search the entire internet?

No. Search coverage is limited to configured public-source providers and depends on provider access, rate limits and availability. ADVERSE is not a universal web crawler and does not guarantee exhaustive results.

Do links or relationship graphs prove something is true?

No. A reachable URL is not proof that a page is true, safe, current or endorsed. A graph connection is an investigative navigation aid, not proof of a real-world relationship. Researchers should review the underlying evidence and original sources.

Who created ADVERSE?

ADVERSE is created by Adeniran Victor Adebowale, who publishes software under the developer identity AD3N1X. It is an independent project maintained in the official ADVERSE source repository.
